    Listen

    Hear two parts of the pipeline with Fortell's published values. Use headphones.

    Compression at 13 ms / 470 ms

    The manual gives an attack of 13 ms and a release of 470 ms. After a loud clap, the background dips, then swells back. Reviewers call this “pumping”.

    Input levelOutput level

    Your own voice in a closed dome

    A closed dome seals the ear. Your skull shakes the trapped air, so your own voice booms. The aid cannot fix it with EQ, because that sound never goes through the aid.

    Your voice at the eardrum vs. an open ear

    “Closed + cancel” needs a mic inside the ear canal. The manual lists none, so Fortell cannot do this today. Values are typical, not Fortell's. Voice clips: ASVspoof 2019 (ODC-By).
